Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Overview

GBRO ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. By using GBRO, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

When you sign up and use GBRO, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (or the equivalent credential from Google Sign-In)
  • Profile data — age, training style, experience level, preferred schedule, and the short goal statement you write during onboarding
  • Gym information — the specific gym you select from our curated list, stored as a unique identifier
  • Connection and chat data — training partner requests you send or receive, whether they were accepted or declined, and messages exchanged in any resulting chat thread
  • Usage data — standard server and browser logs, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically when you access GBRO

We do not collect or store profile photographs. That is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight.

How we use your information

  • To match you with training partners at your gym who share your goals and schedule
  • To operate the connection request and real-time chat features
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session across devices
  • To respond to support requests or safety reports
  • To improve and maintain the platform

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to serve you advertisements. We do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you are using GBRO at a physical gym facility, that gym's own terms of service and privacy policy are always in effect in addition to this policy. GBRO does not supersede, override, or limit any privacy obligations or conduct standards imposed by your gym. You are a member of that facility independently of your GBRO account, and your behavior and personal data at that location remain subject to that gym's rules and policies at all times.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google. We apply security rules at the database level that restrict who can read or write each document — for example, your profile can only be viewed by users who belong to the same gym. We do not store passwords in plain text; authentication is handled directly by Firebase Auth.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and associated data will be removed from our active database. Residual copies may persist in backups for a limited period before being purged.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights including the ability to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To make any such request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

Children

GBRO is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will terminate that account immediately and delete any associated data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of GBRO after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to us at privacy@gbro.app. If you have a safety concern, please use the in-app report feature or contact us directly.
